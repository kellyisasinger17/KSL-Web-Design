Our story

A little history,
a little romance.

You know the feeling.

You'd happily spend an afternoon wandering through an old bookshop, have strong opinions about your favorite period dramas, and occasionally wonder why we ever stopped writing love letters. Maybe you've fallen in love with a fictional character from two hundred years ago. (No judgment here.)

I've always been fascinated by the classics, history, and mythology of many cultures. Cédille & Co. is about making that old-world beauty accessible: inspired by the past, but very much in the present. Each design begins with a story, a historical detail, or simply the thought that someone, somewhere, might have worn something like this a century ago. Let's dust off our velvet-lined jewelry boxes, girls.

With love,
Shay
