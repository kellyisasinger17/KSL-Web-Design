Refund & Return Policy

At Cédille & Co. Jewelry, we want you to love your jewelry as much as we love creating it. If your purchase isn't quite right, we're happy to help.

Returns

We accept returns within 30 days of delivery.

To be eligible for a return, your jewelry must be unworn, unused, and in its original condition, with all original packaging included.

To request a return, please use our Contact Form and include your order number and reason for returning.

Once your return is approved, we'll provide instructions on how and where to send your item. Returns sent without prior authorization will not be accepted.

Damaged or Incorrect Items

Please inspect your jewelry upon delivery. If your order arrives damaged, defective, or incorrect, contact us as soon as possible through our Contact Form, including your order number and photographs of the issue.

We'll review the situation and work with you to make it right.

Non-Returnable Items

The following items are not eligible for return or exchange:

  • Final sale or clearance items
  • Gift cards
  • Custom or personalized jewelry

These exclusions do not affect any rights you may have under applicable consumer protection laws.

Exchanges

We accept exchanges within 30 days of delivery, provided the jewelry meets the eligibility requirements outlined in our return policy.

To request an exchange, use our Contact Form and include your order number and the item you'd like to exchange.

Exchanges are subject to product availability. Because some of our designs are produced in limited quantities, we cannot guarantee that a particular style will be available for exchange.

Refunds

Once we receive and inspect your returned jewelry, we'll notify you whether your refund has been approved.

Approved refunds will be issued to your original payment method within 10 business days. Please allow additional time for your bank or credit card provider to process and post the refund.

Original shipping charges are non-refundable unless the return is due to an error on our part or otherwise required by law.

If more than 15 business days have passed since your refund was approved, please contact us through our Contact Form.

Questions?

We're always happy to help. For questions about returns, exchanges, or your order, please reach out through our Contact Form.

Thank you for supporting Cédille & Co. Jewelry.
