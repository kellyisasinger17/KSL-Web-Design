Privacy Policy

Handling data with care.

This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how it is used, and the choices you have regarding your information when visiting our website.

Last updated: November 6, 2026

Overview

This Privacy Policy explains how Cédille & Co. Jewelry (“Cédille & Co.,” “we,” “us,” or “our”) collects, uses, stores, and shares personal information when you visit our website, browse our jewelry collections, make a purchase, subscribe to our communications, or otherwise interact with us (collectively, the “Services”).

Our online store is powered by Shopify, which provides the ecommerce platform that allows us to offer our jewelry and services.

By using our website, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. Where required by law, we will obtain your consent before collecting or using information for particular purposes.

I. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically to reflect changes to our business, technology, services, or applicable laws.

When changes are made, we will publish the revised policy on our website and update the “Last updated” date above. We will provide additional notice or request consent when required by law.

II. Personal Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you interact with Cédille & Co. Jewelry.

Information You Provide Directly

When you place an order, create an account, contact us, or subscribe to our communications, we may collect:

  • Contact information: Your name, email address, telephone number, and mailing address.
  • Order information: Products purchased, order history, billing and shipping addresses, payment confirmation, and transaction details.
  • Account information: Login credentials and account preferences, where customer accounts are available.
  • Customer service information: Messages, inquiries, photographs, and other information you choose to share when contacting us.
  • Marketing preferences: Newsletter subscriptions and your preferences for receiving promotional communications.
  • Reviews and feedback: Product reviews, ratings, and other content you voluntarily submit.

You may choose not to provide certain information, although doing so may prevent us from completing an order or providing a requested service.

Information Collected Automatically

When you visit our website, certain information may be collected automatically through cookies and similar technologies. This may include:

  • IP address and approximate geographic location.
  • Browser type, device information, and operating system.
  • Pages visited and time spent on our website.
  • Products viewed or added to your shopping cart.
  • Referral sources and interactions with website features.
  • General website performance and usage information.

This information helps us understand how visitors use our website and improve their shopping experience.

Information from Third Parties

We may receive information from third-party services that support our business, including:

  • Shopify and its ecommerce services.
  • Payment processing providers.
  • Shipping and fulfillment companies.
  • Website analytics providers.
  • Email marketing platforms.
  • Advertising and social media services, where enabled.

Information received from these providers is handled in accordance with this Privacy Policy and applicable law.

III. How We Use Your Personal Information

We collect and use personal information for legitimate business purposes, including the following.

Processing and Fulfilling Orders

We use customer information to:

  • Process purchases and payments.
  • Prepare and ship orders.
  • Send order confirmations and delivery updates.
  • Manage returns, exchanges, and refunds.
  • Respond to questions about purchases.
  • Maintain records of transactions.

Customer Service

We use your information to respond to inquiries, resolve problems, provide product information, and assist with orders.

Marketing and Communications

If you subscribe to our newsletter or otherwise authorize marketing communications, we may use your information to share:

  • New jewelry collections and product releases.
  • Special offers and promotions.
  • Brand news and announcements.
  • Stories and inspiration behind our designs.

You may unsubscribe from promotional emails at any time using the unsubscribe link included in those messages. Unsubscribing from marketing emails will not prevent you from receiving essential transactional communications relating to your purchases.

Website Improvement and Analytics

We may use website activity and shopping information to understand customer preferences, improve navigation, evaluate product interest, and enhance our services.

Security, Fraud Prevention, and Legal Compliance

We may use personal information to detect and prevent fraudulent transactions, unauthorized activity, and other potential security risks, and when necessary to comply with applicable laws, maintain business records, resolve disputes, or enforce our policies.

IV. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Like many online stores, Cédille & Co. Jewelry uses cookies and similar technologies to support website functionality and improve the shopping experience.

These technologies may be used to:

  • Remember shopping cart contents and customer preferences.
  • Maintain website security.
  • Support checkout functionality.
  • Understand website traffic and performance.
  • Measure the effectiveness of marketing campaigns.
  • Provide personalized content or advertising, where enabled.

Some cookies are essential for the operation of our website, while others may be used for analytics or marketing.

You can manage certain cookie preferences through your browser settings or through any cookie preference controls made available on our website. Disabling certain cookies may affect website functionality.

For additional information, please review Shopify's Cookie Policy. Where required by law, we will request consent before placing nonessential cookies or using certain tracking technologies.

V. How We Share Personal Information

We do not sell customer contact lists to data brokers. However, we may share personal information with trusted third parties when necessary to operate our business and provide our services.

Ecommerce and payment providers: Shopify and authorized payment processors that securely process transactions and support our online store.

Shipping and fulfillment providers: Carriers and fulfillment partners that require delivery information to ship customer orders.

Jewelry production partners: Manufacturers or service providers who assist with fulfilling orders, particularly when a product requires customization or direct fulfillment.

Technology and analytics providers: Services that support website hosting, performance monitoring, security, and analytics.

Marketing and advertising partners: Platforms used for email communications, advertising, or campaign measurement, where applicable.

Professional advisers and legal authorities: Accountants, legal advisers, regulators, or law enforcement when disclosure is reasonably necessary or legally required.

We may also disclose information in connection with a business transfer, merger, restructuring, or sale, subject to applicable law.

Certain advertising or analytics disclosures may qualify as “sharing,” “sale,” or targeted advertising under particular privacy laws, even when no money is exchanged for personal information. Where applicable, we will provide the required notices and opt-out mechanisms.

VI. Shopify and Payment Security

Our online store operates using Shopify's ecommerce platform. Shopify and its payment processing partners may collect and process information necessary to complete purchases, including payment card details, billing information, and transaction data.

Payment information is processed through authorized payment providers. Cédille & Co. Jewelry does not ordinarily receive or store full payment card numbers through its standard Shopify checkout.

For more information, please review Shopify's Privacy Policy.

VII. Third-Party Websites and Social Media

Our website may contain links to external websites, social media platforms, or third-party services, including Instagram, Pinterest, and other platforms where we share jewelry photography, collection announcements, and brand content.

If you interact with an embedded social media feed or follow a link to another website, that platform may collect information according to its own privacy practices. We encourage you to review the privacy policies of any third-party services you use.

Cédille & Co. Jewelry is not responsible for the independent privacy practices of websites or services we do not control.

VIII. Customer Reviews and Public Content

Our website may allow customers to submit reviews, ratings, photographs, or other content. If you choose to publish information in a publicly visible area of our website, that content may be accessible to other visitors.

Please avoid including sensitive personal information in public reviews or comments. We may moderate submitted content to maintain the safety, accuracy, and integrity of our website.

IX. Data Security

We take reasonable steps to protect the personal information entrusted to us. Our website uses Shopify and other service providers that implement security measures designed to protect customer and transaction information.

However, no method of electronic transmission or storage is completely secure. We encourage customers to use secure networks, protect account credentials, and avoid sending sensitive payment information through ordinary email.

If you believe your information has been compromised, please reach out through our Contact Form.

X. Retention of Personal Information

We retain personal information only for as long as reasonably necessary to fulfill the purposes described in this Privacy Policy. Retention periods may depend on:

  • Order fulfillment and customer service requirements.
  • Accounting and tax obligations.
  • Applicable legal requirements.
  • Fraud prevention and security.
  • Dispute resolution.
  • Customer account management.

When information is no longer required, we will delete, anonymize, or otherwise handle it in accordance with applicable law and our data retention practices.

XI. Your Privacy Rights

Depending on your location, you may have certain rights regarding your personal information. These may include:

  • Access: Request information about the personal data we hold about you.
  • Correction: Request corrections to inaccurate personal information.
  • Deletion: Request deletion of eligible personal information.
  • Portability: Request a copy of certain personal information in a usable format.
  • Opt-out: Opt out of certain targeted advertising, sales, or sharing of personal information where applicable.
  • Restriction: Request restrictions on certain processing activities where applicable.
  • Withdrawal of consent: Withdraw consent for processing activities that rely on consent.
  • Appeal: Appeal certain decisions concerning your privacy requests where applicable law provides that right.

These rights are subject to applicable laws and exceptions. To submit a privacy-related request, please use our Contact Form. We may need to verify your identity before fulfilling certain requests.

We will not discriminate against customers for exercising privacy rights protected by law.

XII. Marketing Preferences

You may unsubscribe from our marketing emails at any time by selecting the unsubscribe link at the bottom of a promotional email. You may also use our Contact Form to request removal from our marketing list.

Transactional emails, such as order confirmations, shipping updates, and important customer service communications, may still be sent when necessary.

XIII. Children's Privacy

Our website is intended for a general audience and is not directed toward children under the age of 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information directly from children under 13.

If we become aware that personal information has been collected from a child in violation of applicable law, we will take appropriate steps to delete it. Parents or guardians who believe their child has provided personal information may reach out through our Contact Form.

XIV. International Visitors

Cédille & Co. Jewelry operates from the United States. If you visit our website from another country, your personal information may be transferred to, stored in, or processed in the United States or other countries where our service providers operate.

These countries may have different data protection laws than your country of residence. Where applicable law requires additional safeguards for international data transfers, we will implement appropriate measures.

XV. Privacy Complaints

If you have questions or concerns about how we collect, use, or protect your information, please reach out through our Contact Form.

We will review privacy-related complaints and respond as required by applicable law. Depending on your location, you may also have the right to submit a complaint to an appropriate privacy or data protection authority.

XVI. Changes to Our Privacy Practices

As Cédille & Co. Jewelry grows, we may introduce new website features, marketing tools, or customer services.

If these changes materially affect how personal information is collected or used, we will update this Privacy Policy and provide additional notice or obtain consent where required.

XVII. Contact Us

For questions about this Privacy Policy, requests concerning your personal information, or other privacy-related inquiries, please reach out to us through our Contact Form.

We appreciate the trust you place in our small business and are committed to handling your information thoughtfully and responsibly.
